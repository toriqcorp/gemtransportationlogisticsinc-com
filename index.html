About Us

Built in Fairburn, growing across the Southeast.

GEM Transportation & Logistics Inc is a regional carrier and logistics coordination company based just south of Atlanta in Fairburn, Georgia. What started as a small dry-van operation has grown into a dependable freight partner for shippers across Georgia, Florida, Alabama, Tennessee, South Carolina, and North Carolina — with select OTR lanes reaching across the lower 48.

We run a company-maintained fleet, keep freight no-touch wherever possible, and believe a driver who's treated right stays for years, not months. Dispatch is local, pay is on time, and equipment is kept in the shop, not held together with tape.
